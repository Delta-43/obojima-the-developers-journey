# Phase 7 · Step 2 — Retrospective & Next Steps

**Goal:** Close out the course with a written retrospective, and decide (and record) what, if anything, comes next.

## Why it matters
`IDEA.md` frames this whole project as a public-facing learning journal. A retrospective is what turns a pile of commits into a story other people (and future you) can actually learn from.

## Concepts to research
- Nothing new technically — this step is about reflection and technical writing

## Deliverable
A root `RETROSPECTIVE.md` covering:
- What you built, phase by phase, with links to the relevant `course_plan` steps
- What was harder or easier than expected, and why
- Concrete things you'd do differently if starting over (schema decisions, stack choices, sequencing)
- A prioritized "next steps" list — e.g. more ingredients/potions, a richer inventory/economy system, better ML features, a proper CI pipeline, more thorough test coverage, accessibility audit, etc.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Every phase of the course is referenced at least once
- [ ] At least 3 concrete lessons learned are documented, specific to this project (not generic platitudes)
- [ ] `Progress.md` is fully up to date, with every completed step checked off
- [ ] The `README.md` at the repo root accurately reflects the finished state of the project and links to this retrospective

## Hints
- Be honest about what you'd change — the value of a public dev journal comes from candor, not a polished highlight reel.
